Before you order

How do I know this gummy actually contains creatine?

Every batch is tested against ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation — a real, independently audited lab standard, not a self-issued badge — and the formulation is built specifically around keeping creatine suspended rather than dissolved, which is the step where most gummies lose their dose.

Will this bloat me?

A standard 3–5g daily dose of creatine — with no loading phase — isn't associated with the water retention people call "bloat." If you've had GI discomfort from powder before, better dissolution may help, though that's a modest, individual effect rather than a guarantee.

Do I need a loading phase?

No. Four gummies daily (5g) is the maintenance dose used in most research. Saturation typically builds over 3–4 weeks either way.

How is this different from other creatine gummies?

Most gummies dissolve creatine directly into the mixture, which is exactly the state where it starts converting to inactive creatinine. Arq8 is formulated to keep creatine suspended rather than dissolved.

Is it vegan and free of artificial ingredients?

Yes. Non-GMO, vegan, sugar-free (sweetened with allulose and monk fruit), with no artificial colors or flavors.

What if it doesn't work for me?

30-day money-back guarantee, no conditions. Subscriptions can be paused or cancelled anytime from your account.
